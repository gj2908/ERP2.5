Introduction

Welcome to the Student Management System (SMS 2.5) User Manual. This comprehensive system streamlines academic institution management by providing specialized interfaces for administrators, staff, and students. This manual introduces key features and provides step-by-step instructions for each user role.

Purpose of the System

The Student Management System enables educational institutions to: - Manage courses, subjects, and classes - Track staff and student information - Record and monitor attendance - Manage student feedback and leave requests - Conduct online classes - Generate reports and analytics

User Roles

The system supports three primary user roles: 1. Admin/HOD (Head of Department): Manages all aspects of the system, including user accounts, courses, classes, staff, and students. 2. Staff: Manages classes, attendance, results, and interacts with students. 3. Students: View attendance, results, provide feedback, apply for leave, and attend online classes.

System Requirements

Hardware Requirements

  • Computer with internet connection
  • Minimum 4GB RAM (8GB recommended)
  • 2GHz dual-core processor or better
  • 10GB available disk space

Software Requirements

  • Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari)
  • JavaScript enabled
  • Cookies enabled
  • Internet connection with minimum 1 Mbps speed

Getting Started

Accessing the System

  1. Open your web browser and navigate to the system URL provided by your administrator.
  2. You will see the login page with the SRMIST logo and login form.

Login Process

  1. Enter your email address in the “Email Address” field.
  2. Enter your password in the “Password” field.
  3. Solve the simple math captcha.
  4. Click “Sign In” to access your dashboard.

Password Recovery

If you forget your password: 1. Click “Forgot your password?” on the login page. 2. Enter your registered email address. 3. Check your email for the password reset link. 4. Click the link and create a new password.

First-time Login

For first-time users, you might need to: 1. Use the temporary credentials provided by your administrator. 2. Change your password immediately after logging in. 3. Update your profile information.

Admin/HOD User Guide

As an Admin/HOD, you have complete control over the Student Management System. This section provides detailed instructions for managing the system.

Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you’ll see the Admin Dashboard, which displays: - Quick statistics (total students, staff, courses, subjects) - Recent activities - System notifications - Quick access to key features

User Management

Managing Admin Accounts

  1. Navigate to “Developer Options” in the System Settings section of the sidebar.
  2. Toggle “Admin Signup” to enable/disable account creation.
  3. Use the Admin Signup Link to create additional admin accounts when needed.

Managing Staff

  1. Adding Staff:
    • Go to “Add Staff” under Staff Management.
    • Fill in the required information (name, email, password, address).
    • Click “Add Staff” to create the account.
  2. Managing Staff:
    • Go to “Manage Staff” under Staff Management.
    • View all staff members in a tabular format.
    • Edit, delete, or update staff information as needed.
  3. Bulk Upload Staff:
    • Go to “Bulk Upload” under Staff Management.
    • Download the sample CSV template.
    • Fill the template with staff information.
    • Upload the file to create multiple staff accounts at once.

Managing Students

  1. Adding Students:
    • Go to “Add Student” under Student Management.
    • Fill in the required information (name, email, password, gender, course, session year).
    • Upload a profile picture if required.
    • Click “Add Student” to create the account.
  2. Managing Students:
    • Go to “Manage Students” under Student Management.
    • View all students in a tabular format.
    • Edit, delete, or update student information as needed.
  3. Bulk Upload Students:
    • Go to “Bulk Upload” under Student Management.
    • Download the sample CSV template.
    • Fill the template with student information.
    • Upload the file to create multiple student accounts at once.

Academic Management

Course Management

  1. Adding Courses:
    • Go to “Add Course” under Course Management.
    • Enter the course name and click “Add Course”.
  2. Managing Courses:
    • Go to “Manage Courses” under Course Management.
    • View, edit, or delete existing courses.
  3. Course Sessions:
    • Go to “Add Session To Course” to assign an academic session to a course.
    • Go to “Manage Course Sessions” to view and manage existing course-session pairs.

Subject Management

  1. Adding Subjects:
    • Go to “Add Subject” under Subject Management.
    • Select a course session and enter the subject name.
    • Click “Add Subject” to create it.
  2. Managing Subjects:
    • Go to “Manage Subjects” under Subject Management.
    • View, edit, or delete existing subjects.

Class Management

  1. Managing Classes:
    • Go to “Manage Classes” under Subject Management.
    • Create new classes by selecting session, course, and section.
    • Assign subjects and staff to classes.
    • Add students to classes.

Session Management

  1. Adding Sessions:
    • Go to “Add Session” under Session Management.
    • Enter the start year and end year.
    • Click “Add Session Year” to create it.
  2. Managing Sessions:
    • Go to “Manage Sessions” under Session Management.
    • View, edit, or delete existing sessions.

Monitoring & Communication

Attendance Management

  1. View attendance records by navigating to “View Attendance”.
  2. Select a class, subject, and date range to view attendance reports.
  3. Export attendance data as Excel or PDF for record-keeping.

Leave Management

  1. Student Leave:
    • Go to “Student Leave” under Feedback & Leave.
    • View and manage student leave requests.
    • Approve or reject requests with a click.
  2. Staff Leave:
    • Go to “Staff Leave” under Feedback & Leave.
    • View and manage staff leave requests.
    • Approve or reject requests with a click.

Feedback Management

  1. Student Feedback:
    • Go to “Student Feedback” under Feedback & Leave.
    • View student feedback and respond if needed.
  2. Staff Feedback:
    • Go to “Staff Feedback” under Feedback & Leave.
    • View staff feedback and respond if needed.

System Configuration

Environment Settings

  1. Navigate to “Developer Options” in the System Settings section.
  2. Configure email settings, debug mode, and domain name.
  3. View other system settings (read-only).

Signup Configuration

  1. Navigate to “Developer Options” in the System Settings section.
  2. Toggle the switches to enable/disable Admin, Staff, and Student signup options.
  3. Use the links to access the signup pages when needed.

Staff User Guide

As a Staff member, you are responsible for managing classes, recording attendance, grading students, and more. This section provides detailed instructions for your role.

Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you’ll see the Staff Dashboard, which displays: - Your assigned subjects and classes - Quick statistics (total students, subjects, online classes) - Recent activities - System notifications

Profile Management

  1. Viewing Your Profile:
    • Click on your name in the top-right corner.
    • Select “Profile” from the dropdown menu.
    • View your personal information.
  2. Changing Password:
    • Click on your name in the top-right corner.
    • Select “Change Password” from the dropdown menu.
    • Enter your current and new passwords.
    • Click “Save” to update your password.

Class Management

Taking Attendance

  1. Go to “Take Attendance” in the sidebar.
  2. Select the subject, class, and date.
  3. Mark attendance for each student (Present/Absent).
  4. Click “Save Attendance” to record.

Updating Attendance

  1. Go to “Update Attendance” in the sidebar.
  2. Select the subject, class, and date.
  3. Modify the attendance records as needed.
  4. Click “Update Attendance” to save changes.

Conducting Online Classes

  1. Starting a Live Class:
    • Go to “Start Live Class” in the sidebar.
    • Select the subject and session year.
    • Enter room name and password.
    • Click “Start Class” to create the online classroom.
    • Share the link with students.
  2. Managing Active Classes:
    • Go to “View Active Classes” to see your ongoing classes.
    • End classes when completed.
    • Export attendance reports.
  3. Generating Class Links:
    • Go to “Generate Class Link”.
    • Select the subject and class.
    • Set an expiration date for the link.
    • Share the generated code with students.

Student Assessment

Managing Results

  1. Adding Results:
    • Go to “Add Result” in the sidebar.
    • Select the class, subject, and student.
    • Enter exam and assignment marks.
    • Click “Save Result” to record.
  2. Viewing Results:
    • Go to “View Result” in the sidebar.
    • Select the class and subject to view all student results.
    • Edit results if needed.

Leave Management

  1. Applying for Leave:
    • Go to “Apply Leave” in the sidebar.
    • Enter the leave date(s) and reason.
    • Click “Apply for Leave” to submit your request.
  2. Viewing Leave Status:
    • Go to “Apply Leave” to see your leave history.
    • Check the status of your leave requests (Pending/Approved/Rejected).

Feedback

  1. Submitting Feedback:
    • Go to “Feedback” in the sidebar.
    • Enter your feedback message.
    • Click “Submit Feedback” to send.
  2. Viewing Responses:
    • Go to “Feedback” to see your feedback history.
    • Check if there are any replies from the administration.

Troubleshooting

Common Issues and Solutions

  1. Cannot Log In
    • Ensure you’re using the correct email and password.
    • Check if Caps Lock is on.
    • Use the “Forgot Password” feature to reset your password.
    • Contact the administrator if problems persist.
  2. Session Timeout
    • The system will automatically log you out after 30 minutes of inactivity.
    • Save your work frequently.
    • Log back in to continue your work.
  3. Cannot Join Online Class
    • Ensure you’ve entered the correct class code.
    • Check your internet connection.
    • Make sure your browser supports video conferencing.
    • Allow camera and microphone permissions when prompted.
  4. Cannot View Attendance or Results
    • Ensure you have access to the selected class/subject.
    • Try refreshing the page.
    • Clear your browser cache.
    • Contact your teacher or administrator if problems persist.
  5. File Upload Issues
    • Ensure the file size is within limits (usually under 5MB).
    • Check if the file format is supported.
    • Try a different browser if issues persist.
